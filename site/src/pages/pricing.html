Pricing questions

Is the free version limited?

No. Everything in the free plugin is fully working, with no locked features, trials or licence checks. Pro is a separate add-on for stores that need combined rules, bulk changes, overrides, backups or WP All Import.

Does BulkTab Pro renew automatically?

Yes. The licence renews automatically each year at $49.99 until you cancel, and you can cancel any time from your Freemius account. If you cancel or the licence lapses, every Pro feature keeps working on your site; only updates and email support stop.

How many sites can I use Pro on?

One. Each BulkTab Pro licence covers 1 site, and that site also needs the free BulkTab plugin installed.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. There is a 30-day no-questions refund. Email us within 30 days of buying.

What happens to my tabs if my licence expires?

Nothing. Your tabs and Pro features keep working. Only updates and support stop.

Who handles the payment?

BulkTab Pro is sold through Freemius, the merchant of record, which runs the checkout, the payment and your licence key. Nothing from Freemius loads until you click a buy button; the checkout then opens on this page, or on the Freemius site if JavaScript is off.
