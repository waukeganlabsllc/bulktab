For how Freemius handles this data and your rights, see the Freemius privacy policy.

  • Nothing loads until you click Buy. No page on this site, including the pricing page, contacts Freemius or any other third party when it loads.

The BulkTab plugin

  • The free plugin does no tracking and sends no data anywhere. Your tabs stay in your WordPress database.
  • BulkTab Pro uses the Freemius SDK to activate your licence and deliver updates. That exchange with Freemius is covered by Freemius's privacy policy.
  • Deleting the plugin keeps your tab data unless you choose to delete it under Products → Tab settings.
